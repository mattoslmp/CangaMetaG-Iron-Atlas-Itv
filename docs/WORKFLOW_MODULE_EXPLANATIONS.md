# Atlas workflow and module explanations

The workflow is displayed immediately below the article abstract in the app. Each module is linked to the analyses performed and the figures or outputs generated.

## Article Atlas / Overview

**Analyses performed:** Summarizes the study, sampling context, citations, core counts and database traceability.

**Figures and outputs generated:** Article summary card, workflow schematic, citation and inventory panels.

## Taxonomic profiles

**Analyses performed:** Loads Kaiju-derived domain, phylum, family, genus and species tables; compares lake, season and omics-layer profiles; displays richness/diversity and NMDS outputs.

**Figures and outputs generated:** Figures 2-4; Supplementary Figures 1-4, 14, 19-29, 32 and 40.

## MAGs and genomes

**Analyses performed:** Reports 50 recovered MAGs, quality metrics, GTDB-Tk/BV-BRC/Kaiju/rRNA annotations, FASTA/GBK availability and antiSMASH BGC summaries.

**Figures and outputs generated:** Figure 5; Supplementary Figures 5, 17-18 and antiSMASH/MAG tables.

## KO biomarkers and differential abundance

**Analyses performed:** Integrates curated KO biomarkers for carbon, methane, nitrogen, sulfur, oxygenic photosynthesis and iron metabolism; performs dry/rainy differential and directional contrast summaries.

**Figures and outputs generated:** Figures 6-8; Supplementary Figures 6-12, 33-37.

## KEGG/KEMET modules

**Analyses performed:** Parses KEMET reportKMC outputs and consolidated MAG/metagenome workbooks to score complete, incomplete and absent KEGG modules and module KO components.

**Figures and outputs generated:** Supplementary Figures 38-39 and KEGG module completeness heatmaps.

## Iron-rich environment comparison

**Analyses performed:** Uses Supplementary Table 8 and IMG/M metadata to compare Amazonian lateritic lake profiles with external iron-rich environments using descriptive log2-ratio contrasts and z-score heatmaps.

**Figures and outputs generated:** Supplementary Figures 33-37; ST8 heatmaps and metadata panels.

## Environmental integrator

**Analyses performed:** Combines sample coordinates, dates and optional external environmental layers from Copernicus/Sentinel, NASA Earthdata/CMR, CHIRPS, MapBiomas and SoilGrids when credentials/data are available.

**Figures and outputs generated:** Coordinate maps, environmental tables, downloaded-layer summaries and cache/audit outputs.

## Code and reproducibility

**Analyses performed:** Indexes scripts, source data, figure-generation code, package manifests, analysis environments and downloadable audit tables.

**Figures and outputs generated:** Workflow figure, figure-script manifest, methods tables and reproducibility inventory.
